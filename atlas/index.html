Verification lab

Trust is a process, not a badge.

⌕

1. Provenance

Preserve original source, permissions, timestamp, method, and transformations. Separate first-hand observation from hearsay.

⟁

2. Method review

Check whether measurements support the claim. Surface missing baselines, confounders, selection effects, and weak comparisons.

⟳

3. Independent replication

Seek independent observations across sites and populations. Replication strengthens a claim but does not erase context.

⚖

4. Contradiction handling

Keep disagreement visible. Link conflicting evidence, track revisions, and show where a claim holds or remains uncertain.

Trust & principles

Legitimacy depends on evidence quality and people's rights.

◉

Truth isn't popularity

Votes and prestige cannot substitute for provenance, methods, replication, or transparent uncertainty.

♧

Credit contributors

Respect consent, attribution, community data rights, and benefit sharing. Local knowledge holders are collaborators.

⌘

Open, private by design

Publish reusable evidence where safe. Protect personal, sensitive, proprietary, and community-restricted information.

⟲

Correction is a feature

Claims can be challenged, corrected, scoped down, or withdrawn. Preserve auditable revision history.
