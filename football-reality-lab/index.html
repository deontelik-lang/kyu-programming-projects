MODEL NOTES

Ball-flight model

Forces and integration
↗
Gravity

Acceleration downward is approximately g = 9.81 m/s². Position updates from velocity over a small time step: xₜ₊Δt ≈ xₜ + vₜΔt.

≈
Quadratic air drag

Fᵈ = −½ρCᵈA|vᵣ|vᵣ. Air density ρ, drag coefficient Cᵈ, cross-sectional area A and relative air velocity vᵣ determine slowing.

⟳
Magnus effect

A spinning ball experiences a lift force approximately proportional to ρ, radius, spin and speed. A calibrated model can use Fₘ ∝ ω × v; coefficients depend on regime and ball.

⌁
Ground collision

Normal rebound is approximated by v′ₙ = −e vₙ, where e is restitution. Tangential friction changes with wetness, grass, mud, ball speed and contact.

⚽
Boot-to-ball impulse

Impulse J = ∫F dt changes momentum: m(v′ − v) = J. Contact point, foot speed, angle, spin and impact duration affect launch.

Human & body model

Stateful agents and biomechanical constraints
◎
Decision under uncertainty

Players evaluate imperfectly perceived options. Skill, vision, pressure, confidence and fatigue alter choice quality and reaction time; errors remain possible.

↯
Momentum & contact

F = ma relates net force to acceleration. Body mass, posture, balance and contact geometry constrain turns, shielding, tackles and collisions.

♥
Fatigue & recovery

Metabolic demand accumulates by work intensity and duration. Recovery depends on rest, prior load, conditioning, heat and individual physiology—not a flat stat penalty.

◉
Injury risk

A production model would estimate exposure from load spikes, tissue capacity, fatigue and contact, with uncertainty. It must not pretend to diagnose real medical outcomes.

♧
Crowd feedback

Home support influences perceived pressure and emotional arousal. Its effect varies by player, match state, culture and experience; it never dictates a result.

Current prototype: interactive tactical animation + event-generation model. It does not yet calculate true 3D ball aerodynamics, deformable turf, net cloth, skeletal biomechanics or validated injury probabilities.
